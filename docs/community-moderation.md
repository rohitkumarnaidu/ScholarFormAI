# Community Moderation Policy

## Code of Conduct Enforcement

ScholarFormAI is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone. This policy outlines how the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) is enforced.

All community members are expected to:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

## Moderation Team

The moderation team consists of:

- **Project Lead** — oversees all moderation decisions
- **Moderators** (3-5 members) — handle day-to-day enforcement
- **Community Managers** — handle outreach, warnings, and education

Moderators are selected by the project lead from active, respected community members. Terms are 12 months, renewable.

## Reporting Process

### How to Report

1. **Direct message** a moderator on Discord/Slack
2. **Email** moderation@scholarform.ai
3. **GitHub** — use the `@moderators` team mention for urgent issues

### What to Include

- Your contact info (optional for anonymous reports)
- The offending user/content (link, screenshot, quote)
- The specific rule violated (from Code of Conduct)
- Date, time, and context
- Any supporting evidence

Reports are acknowledged within 24 hours and investigated within 72 hours.

## Moderation Actions

| Action | Description | Duration | Appealable |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------- | ------------ |
| **Warning** | Formal notice with explanation of the violation | Permanent record | Yes |
| **Temporary Ban** | Removal from community spaces | 7-30 days | Yes |
| **Permanent Ban** | Indefinite removal from all community spaces | Permanent | Yes (after 6 months) |
| **Content Removal** | Deletion of offending posts/comments | Immediate | Yes |

### Escalation

- First violation → Warning
- Second violation → 7-day temporary ban
- Third violation → 30-day temporary ban
- Severe violation (harassment, threats, doxxing) → Permanent ban on first offense

## Appeal Process

1. Submit an appeal via moderation@scholarform.ai within 14 days of the action
2. Include: your name, the action taken, why you believe it was incorrect, and what you will do differently
3. Appeals are reviewed by a moderator not involved in the original decision
4. Decision on appeal is final and issued within 7 days

## Transparency Reports

The moderation team publishes a quarterly transparency report covering:

- Total moderation actions taken
- Breakdown by action type (warning, temp ban, permanent ban)
- Number of appeals received and upheld
- Notable incidents (anonymized)
- Changes to moderation policy

See [Transparency Reports](transparency-reports.md) for the current report and template.
