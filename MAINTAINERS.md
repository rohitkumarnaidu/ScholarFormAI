## Maintainer Responsibilities

1. **Code Review**: Review pull requests within 48 hours where possible
2. **Issue Triage**: Respond to new issues and label appropriately
3. **Release Management**: Participate in release planning and execution
4. **Community**: Engage with the community, answer questions, and moderate discussions
5. **Direction**: Help define and communicate the project roadmap

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions may be nominated for maintainer status.

### Criteria

- Consistent contributions over 6+ months
- Deep understanding of one or more project areas
- Active participation in community discussions
- Demonstrated good judgment in technical decisions
- Alignment with project values and Code of Conduct

### Process

1. Existing maintainer nominates the candidate
2. Discussion period of 2 weeks
3. Vote among current maintainers (2/3 majority required)
4. Candidate accepts or declines

## Maintainer Expectations

- Maintainers are expected to remain active. Inactive maintainers (no contributions for 6+ months) may be moved to emeritus status.
- Maintainers should declare conflicts of interest.
- Maintainers should recuse themselves from votes where they have a conflict.
