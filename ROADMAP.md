# ScholarForm AI Roadmap

Welcome to the **ScholarForm AI Roadmap**. This document outlines our strategic vision, upcoming milestones, and long-term goals for automating academic manuscript formatting and generation using Agentic AI.

As an enterprise-grade open-source platform, our roadmap is guided by community feedback, advancements in multi-agent AI architectures, and the evolving needs of the academic publishing industry.

## Vision Statement
To eliminate formatting friction and repetitive manual labor in academic publishing, allowing researchers to focus entirely on scientific discovery. We achieve this by providing a robust, extensible, and secure platform that leverages state-of-the-art AI.

## Current Status: Version 1.x
Our initial release focuses on core automation and stability:
- **One-Click Formatter:** Support for DOCX, PDF, LaTeX, Markdown, and HTML.
- **Template Support:** 17+ publisher templates (IEEE, APA, Springer, Nature).
- **Agentic AI Workflow:** Generation of research documents from prompts.
- **Enterprise Security:** SLSA Level 3 compliance, RBAC, and rate limiting.

## Upcoming Milestones

### Q3 2026: Version 2.0 (The Collaborative Era)
- **Peer Review Simulations:** Autonomous AI agents that simulate editorial and peer review processes, providing actionable feedback prior to submission.
- **Collaborative Editing (CRDTs):** Real-time multiplayer editing capabilities allowing multiple researchers to co-author manuscripts seamlessly.
- **Enhanced Synthesis Agent:** Improved multi-document RAG capabilities for complex literature reviews.

### Q4 2026: Version 2.5 (Extensibility & Ecosystem)
- **Plugin Architecture:** A robust SDK allowing organizations to write their own custom extraction and formatting plugins.
- **Extended Template Library:** Adding 50+ specialized university and niche journal templates.
- **Advanced Forensic Auditor:** Deeper equation checking, statistical verification, and plagiarism detection integration.

### 2027 and Beyond: Version 3.0 (Global Scale)
- **Multilingual Support:** Native processing, translation, and formatting of manuscripts in over 40 languages.
- **Cloud-Native Deployments:** Turn-key Helm charts and Operator patterns for seamless Kubernetes deployments in private environments.
- **Open Access Integrations:** Direct API integrations with major open access repositories (arXiv, bioRxiv) for automated publishing pipelines.
