# ScholarForm AI — Wiki Home

> Welcome to the ScholarForm AI community wiki — your central hub for everything from first-time setup to production deployment.

**ScholarForm AI** is an open-source platform that automates academic manuscript formatting. Upload a DOCX/PDF manuscript or generate one from scratch via AI, and receive a publisher-ready document in seconds.

- **GitHub:** [https://github.com/rohitkumarnaidu/ScholarFormAI](https://github.com/rohitkumarnaidu/ScholarFormAI)
- **License:** MIT
- **Contact:** `@scholarform.ai`
