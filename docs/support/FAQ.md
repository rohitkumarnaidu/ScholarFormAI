# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about ScholarFormAI.

## General

### What is ScholarFormAI?
ScholarFormAI is an automated tool that formats `.docx` files according to strict academic and scholarly standards (such as APA, MLA, IEEE, Chicago) using advanced document parsing and AI.

### Does this service support PDF files?
Currently, ScholarFormAI only natively ingests `.docx` files. However, it can export formatted results to both `.docx` and `.pdf` formats.

## Account & Billing

### How is API usage billed?
Usage is billed per document processed. For large documents exceeding 100 pages, additional per-page charges may apply. Check your dashboard for detailed billing metrics.

### Do you offer academic discounts?
Yes, researchers and students with a valid `.edu` or institutional email address can apply for our academic discount program.

## Technical

### Are my documents stored on your servers?
We prioritize your privacy. Documents are encrypted at rest during processing and are automatically permanently deleted 24 hours after processing completes, unless you explicitly opt into our training dataset.

### Why did my document fail to format?
Formatting usually fails due to corrupted `.docx` XML structures or heavily nested, complex tables that the parser cannot unravel. Review our [Troubleshooting Guide](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) for more help.
